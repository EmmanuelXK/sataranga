# cburnett chess pieces

These six files (`wK.svg`, `wQ.svg`, `wR.svg`, `wB.svg`, `wN.svg`, `wP.svg`) are the white pieces from the cburnett set, Lichess’s standard chess pieces.

- Artist: Colin M.L. Burnett
- Source: https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett
- The files are unmodified.

They are used only for the chess side of the Challenge tab. The SATARANGA piece art is separate and is not part of this set.
